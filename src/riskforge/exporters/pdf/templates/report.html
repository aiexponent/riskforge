2. Methodology

The assessment uses the RiskForge question bank, version {{ register.question_bank_version }}, across {{ dimensions | length }} risk dimensions: {{ dimensions | map('replace', '_', ' ') | join(', ') }}.

Each risk is scored for likelihood and severity on a five-point scale. The risk score is likelihood times severity, from 1 to 25. Bands: low 1 to 4, medium 5 to 9, high 10 to 16, critical 17 to 25. Likelihood and severity are scored again after mitigation to give the residual score.

A risk is open while its residual score is above the risk appetite threshold of {{ register.risk_appetite_threshold }} and it has not been accepted with a written rationale. Gate G3 fails while any risk is open.

Risk patterns applied: {{ patterns_used | join(', ') if patterns_used else 'none' }}.
